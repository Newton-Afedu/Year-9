Activity 2 · 10 minutes · Group of 3

👥 Who Is Responsible?

Each person in your group chooses a different role. Spend 2 minutes identifying three responsibilities or safe actions. Then teach your group.

📤 Person sharing content

Posts, forwards or distributes a message, photo or video.

What should they think about before pressing Send?

👀 Bystander

Witnesses harmful behaviour but is not directly targeted.

How can they help without escalating the situation?

❤️ Person targeted

Is affected by harmful messages or actions.

What can they do to stay safe and get support?
Reveal key ideas after your discussion

The sharer should obtain consent and avoid spreading harmful content. A bystander can refuse to forward, offer support and report. The targeted person deserves help and is never to blame for being bullied.

Activity 3 · 12 minutes · Team challenge

🧩 Digital Dilemma Challenge

Choose a scenario. Your group must identify the problem, explain each person's responsibilities, suggest three safe actions and identify who can help.

Extension: What changes if the content has already been shared with hundreds of people?
